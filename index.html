Why routines fail (and how Rooted helps)

Inconsistent use

Busy days interrupt complicated routines. A simple checklist makes the next step easier to find.

Open today’s checklist →

Expecting fast results

Daily changes are hard to spot. The fortnightly photo journal helps you look back over time.

Starting later than hoped

People often seek support after noticing changes. This tool sets expectations without predicting anyone’s response.

What to expect

Month 1: build a routine; visible change may not be apparent. Month 2: some people notice shedding, experiences vary. Months 3–4: changes may still be gradual. Months 5–6: review progress and next steps with your clinician. No outcome is guaranteed.
